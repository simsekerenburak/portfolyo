A telescope tracking system built on STM32 that runs in both autonomous and manual modes; it maps the stars and locks onto the selected target (GOTO). A stepper motor drives the horizontal axis and a servo the vertical axis. The ground control station is written in Python/PyQt6 and talks over UART.

My role: computing the sensor data.
