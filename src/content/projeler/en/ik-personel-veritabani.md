A personnel management web application I built during my internship at DOĞAKA (East Mediterranean Development Agency), handed over to the IT team for real use at the agency.

- Node.js and relational SQLite (`node:sqlite`)
- Role-based access control (RBAC) and audit logs
- Soft delete and a recycle bin
- Salted scrypt password storage, brute-force lockout
- Employee self-service change requests and an HR approval flow
- Excel import, PDF/DOCX reporting
- A KVKK-focused (Turkish data protection law) security report
